# Skynet

## Overview

Skynet is an intelligent cyber resilience platform designed to protect digital infrastructure from security threats while ensuring uninterrupted service availability. The system continuously monitors user activities, network behavior, and operational environments to identify suspicious patterns and potential risks before they impact critical services.

## Problem Statement

Modern organizations face increasing challenges from cyber attacks, unauthorized access attempts, system failures, and service disruptions. Existing solutions often focus only on threat detection or only on recovery, resulting in delayed responses and prolonged downtime. There is a need for a unified platform that can both detect threats and respond intelligently to maintain operational continuity.

## Vision

To create a smart and resilient ecosystem capable of identifying threats, assessing risks, and autonomously responding to incidents while maintaining trust, security, and availability.

## Objectives

- Detect abnormal activities and suspicious behavior in real time.
- Analyze security incidents and determine their severity.
- Reduce operational risks through intelligent decision-making.
- Enable automated response mechanisms for common incidents.
- Improve service reliability and cyber resilience.
- Maintain detailed records of security events and actions.

## Core Concepts

### Threat Detection
The system continuously analyzes activities and behaviors to identify unusual patterns that may indicate security threats or operational issues.

### Risk Assessment
Every event is evaluated based on its potential impact and likelihood, allowing the system to prioritize critical incidents.

### Trust Evaluation
Users, devices, and activities are assessed through trust-based mechanisms to determine whether actions should be allowed, verified, or restricted.

### Intelligent Response
When a threat or disruption is detected, the system recommends or initiates appropriate actions to minimize impact and restore normal operations.

### Continuous Monitoring
The platform operates continuously to provide real-time visibility into system health, security posture, and operational status.
