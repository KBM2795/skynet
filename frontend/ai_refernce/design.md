# Skynet Landing Page Design System

## 1. Design Direction

The landing page follows a modern AI SaaS aesthetic focused on clarity, trust, and intelligence, built upon the official **shadcn/ui Luma (`radix-luma`)** design style.

The overall experience should feel:
- Modern and premium
- Data-driven and intelligent
- Professional but approachable
- Clean with generous, breathable whitespace (signature Luma rhythm)
- Soft, fluid geometry with rounded contours and pill-shaped controls
- Focused on conversion and credibility

Visual style combines light enterprise sections with deep blue feature sections to create contrast and highlight key content.
